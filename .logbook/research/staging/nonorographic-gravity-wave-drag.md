# Add Weak Non-Orographic Gravity-Wave Momentum Drag

## Hypothesis

The current `dino_hsl2_mass_dse` incumbent has accepted thermal transport,
surface residual, weak-HS, ocean sensible-heat, and semi-implicit damping
machinery, but it still lacks any representation of momentum deposition by
unresolved non-orographic gravity waves. Operational models use non-orographic
gravity-wave drag because waves generated by convection, fronts, and shear zones
propagate upward and break, depositing momentum in the middle atmosphere. A very
weak upper-layer momentum tendency may reduce slow wind and height drift without
touching the accepted mass-DSE transport, pressure-thickness balance, ocean heat
flux placement, or scored-output residuals.

## Mechanism

Add a side-by-side model, for example `dino_hsl2_mass_dse_ngwd`, derived from
`dino_hsl2_mass_dse`.

Implement one positive-time-only step filter after the normal dynamics,
horizontal diffusion, Coriolis split, and theta recentering filters:

- diagnose nodal wind from the next state's vorticity/divergence;
- compute the column mass-weighted barotropic wind and damp only the upper-level
  baroclinic wind anomaly, not the column-mean momentum;
- apply a fixed smooth vertical taper that is active only above a conservative
  sigma threshold, for example full strength for `sigma <= 0.20`, tapering to
  zero by `sigma >= 0.45`;
- use one fixed long e-folding time, for example 20 forecast days, and cap the
  dimensional wind increment per inner step, for example at 0.15 m/s equivalent;
- transform the corrected wind back to vorticity/divergence and preserve
  temperature, log-surface-pressure, tracers, residual corrections, DFI,
  weak-HS forcing, ocean sensible heat flux, and all HSL/DSE branches;
- exclude the filter from DFI so the time-reversed initialization remains the
  accepted dry balanced path;
- fall back to the incumbent state when wind transforms, tapers, or corrected
  modal fields are nonfinite.

This is intentionally not a new Rayleigh boundary-layer drag or a broad sponge:
it does not act near the surface, does not damp vorticity/divergence directly by
wavenumber, and does not change temperature or mass variables.

## Expected Metric Movement

- Expected improvements:
  - `10m_u_component_of_wind` at medium and late leads if upper-level momentum
    drift projects onto barotropic wind error.
  - `geopotential_500` and `mean_sea_level_pressure` if weaker upper-level shear
    drift improves balanced large-scale height evolution.
- Expected neutral metrics:
  - `2m_temperature`, because no heat source, surface residual, or ocean flux is
    changed.
  - Early leads, because the filter is weak, upper-level only, and excluded from
    DFI.
- Possible regressions:
  - Upper-level drag can alter real Rossby-wave propagation and thermal-wind
    balance.
  - If the incumbent's 10 m wind error is mostly surface-layer diagnostic error,
    upper-layer drag will be neutral or negative.

## Risks

- Numerical stability:
  - Low to moderate. The operation is damping and capped, but it edits prognostic
    momentum each inner step.
- Compute cost:
  - Low. It adds wind transform and inverse-transform work already used elsewhere
    in the adapter.
- Data leakage:
  - None. The filter uses only forecast state, model geometry, and fixed
    literature-scale constants.
- Physical plausibility:
  - Moderate. The process is real, but this candidate is a deliberately reduced
    deterministic bulk drag rather than a spectral source-level parameterization.
- Rollback complexity:
  - Low. Remove one selector/filter, one factory/export, one registry entry, and
    focused tests.

  - A clean near-zero or negative iteration delta would show unresolved

    guardrail failure would show the drag disrupts accepted balance.

## Researcher Notes

This is not a duplicate of the staged boundary-layer drag or Richardson mixing
ideas because it is upper-level and baroclinic-anomaly only. It is not the staged
orographic-wave or mountain-blocking family because it uses no terrain and no
orographic source. It is not the scrapped `upper-sigma-rayleigh-sponge` because
it preserves column-mean momentum and edits reconstructed wind with a physical
upper-layer taper instead of damping all upper-level modes. It avoids the
recently rejected pressure-thickness, hydrostatic inversion, vertical DSE,
DSE-initialization, ocean-depth, and global-remap families entirely.

## Evaluator Notes

### 2026-06-24T18:51:46Z

Decision: move to `staging`; rank 2 of 3 new proposals; ready now: no.

The physical process is real and the citations support non-orographic gravity
wave drag as an operational middle-atmosphere momentum-deposition mechanism.
The proposal is also distinct from terrain-sourced orographic drag and from
lower-boundary Rayleigh or Richardson mixing. That keeps it researchable rather
than an automatic duplicate.

It is not the best next experiment because the local queue already has strong
negative or cautionary evidence for closely related damping/filter mechanisms.
`divergence-selective-gravity-wave-damping` was clean but lost primary score,
`upper-sigma-rayleigh-sponge` was scrapped for under-supported upper-level
damping, and `baroclinic-shear-selective-hyperdiffusion` was scrapped for the
same wind-reconstruction and baroclinic-damping risk this proposal would carry.
This NGWD design is narrower than those files because it preserves column-mean
momentum, but it still reduces to a fixed, source-free upper-level baroclinic
wind drag with arbitrary strength and caps.

Keep staged as a later fallback if diagnostics show persistent upper-level
baroclinic wind drift after the pressure-tide candidate is resolved. Before
promotion it needs a tighter predeclared amplitude rationale or read-only
evidence that upper-level momentum drift materially affects the scored
`10m_u_component_of_wind`, `mean_sea_level_pressure`, or `geopotential_500`
errors under the current `dino_hsl2_mass_dse` incumbent.
