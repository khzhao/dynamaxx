## Implementation Summary

Registered `dinosaur_dfi_surface_residual_weak_hs_logp_init_hydrostatic_layer_init`
as a side-by-side candidate. The factory preserves the incumbent DFI, weak
Held-Suarez relaxation, near-surface residual correction, log-pressure
initialization, T80 defaults, 900 s inner step, vertical advection, spectral
filter, zero-orography runtime path, and unrestricted output-variable contract.

The only forecast-mechanism change is the hydrostatic temperature estimator
used before pressure-to-sigma initialization. The new helper computes adjacent
hypsometric layer-mean virtual temperatures from geopotential thickness in log
pressure, converts layer means to dry temperature using layer-averaged specific
humidity when a complete humidity stack is available, maps layer means back to
pressure levels with single adjacent values at the top and bottom and adjacent
averages in the interior, and falls back pointwise to analyzed temperature for
nonfinite or nonpositive reconstructed values. With fewer than two pressure
levels it returns analyzed temperature.

Focused tests cover analytic adjacent-layer reconstruction, humidity conversion,
pointwise invalid fallback, factory preservation of incumbent flags, dependency
import/export behavior, and registry creation/listing.
